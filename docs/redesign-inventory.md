# Kasa redesign preservation checklist

This checklist records the existing product surface before the full-site redesign. The Welcome and Home approval round is the first completed phase; later screens remain unchanged until approval.

## Global shell

- Desktop sidebar: household/home logo, Viewing as selector, Notifications, Settings, Add menu, Home, Chores, Expenses & Shopping, Calendar, Profile, saved-device status.
- Phone shell: top bar, Notifications, Settings, saved-device/demo controls, bottom navigation, centered Add menu.
- Add menu: chore, expense, shopping item, event.
- Shared UI: dialogs, form validation, menus, confirmations, toasts and undo feedback, coachmarks, storage error.

## Welcome and account entry

- Round table Kasa logo.
- Continue with Google / dummy account entry.
- Try the demo.
- Create your household (offline mode), cancel, name fields, roommate fields, add/remove roommate, starter chore checkboxes, create action.
- Saved on this device disclosure and prototype limitations.
- Signed-in onboarding: import local household, create household, join with invite code, sign out.
- Saved-data recovery screen.

## Home

- Greeting/date, demo status and Viewing as controls.
- Overdue chores, Your chores, upcoming chores, complete/open/menu actions, Add chore, Open chores.
- Past-due household chores and assignees.
- Expenses balance, settlement detail, repayment action, Open expenses.
- Household plans, event detail, Add event, Calendar.
- Shopping list, claim state, Add item, Open list.
- House expectations: preview, expand/collapse, add/edit/remove.
- Shared purchasing rules: budget, preferred products, store preferences, add/remove/change actions.
- Recent activity and links.

## Chores

- My / All / Completed tabs; grouped overdue, today, later this week, next week.
- Add, open, edit, delete, complete, undo, reassign, ask someone to take, offer trade.
- Swap requests: accept, decline, take, cancel.
- Weekly counts, previous/next/current week.
- Chore dialog fields: title, notes, assignee, due date, one-time/weekly, rotation members, auto-advance, cycle length.
- Chore detail dialog and completion history.

## Expenses and Shopping

- Expenses / Shopping tabs.
- Budget & spending card, progress, status, set/change budget.
- Balances, who owes whom, record repayment.
- Expense history: add, open/edit/delete, amount/date/payer/participants and split preview.
- Shopping quick add, quantity, needed/purchased lists, claim, purchase, edit/delete, add again, undo/reopen, linked expense.
- Purchasing guidance.

## Calendar

- Month / Agenda views, previous/next, Today, Add event.
- Month cells, selected day, compact event/chore chips, overflow count.
- Date strip and 14-day agenda.
- Event categories and legend: quiet/study, away, guests, household plans, plus chores.
- Event detail/add/edit/delete with title, notes, category, start/end date and time.

## Profile

- Account card when signed in, email, phone state, sign out.
- Local/synced information banner.
- Dietary preferences, personal monthly budget, personal roommate rules, counters, save.
- Other roommate profiles.

## Notifications

- Unread count, Mark all read, Reminder settings.
- Today / Yesterday / Earlier groups.
- Open notification and mark read/unread menu.
- Empty state and in-app-only note.

## Settings

- Household summary.
- Reminder and update preferences, lead-time selector.
- In-app, push and SMS channel choices and limitations.
- About this prototype.
- Getting-started replay.
- Research event count and JSON export.
- Signed-in account/sign out.
- Danger zone reset/delete.

## Approval gate

- [x] Welcome redesigned at desktop and phone breakpoints without removing controls.
- [x] Home redesigned at desktop and phone breakpoints without removing controls.
- [ ] Remaining screens redesigned after Welcome/Home approval.
- [ ] Final cross-screen control comparison completed.
